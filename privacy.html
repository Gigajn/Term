Privacy Policy

Baca dalam Bahasa Indonesia

This Policy explains what personal data Snouvier collects, why, with whom it is shared, and your rights, in accordance with Indonesian Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection.

1. Our role

For the account data of Merchant Owners and staff, Snouvier is the data controller. For Customer data recorded by a Merchant, such as a buyer's name and phone number, the Merchant is the data controller and Snouvier processes it on the Merchant's behalf.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, business name, outlet address, and password (stored encrypted). If you use Sign in with Google: name, email, and profile photo. We do not access Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google data.
  • Operational data: menus, prices, stock, transactions, shifts and working hours, expenses, credit sales, points, and reports recorded in the app.
  • Customer data recorded by the Merchant: name, phone number, order history, credit sales, and points. If the WhatsApp Assistant is active: the content of Customer messages to the Merchant's WhatsApp number.
  • Customer WhatsApp numbers for WhatsApp Receipts: stored only if the Customer consents, together with a record of who recorded that consent. Without consent, the number is used only on the cashier's phone to open WhatsApp and is not sent to the server.
  • Subscription payment data: invoice status and history. Card numbers and bank details are processed directly by the payment provider and are not stored by us.
  • Device and technical data: device type, app version, IP address, error logs, and security verification (captcha) tokens.
  • Device permissions used only while the related feature is in use: camera (scanning QR codes and photographing receipts or products), Bluetooth (receipt printers), and notification access (only if the Merchant turns on GoPay QRIS Verification, to read payment notifications from the GoPay Merchant app; other notifications are ignored).

3. How data is used

  • to provide the service: sign-in, recording sales, stock, reports, and synchronization across devices;
  • to send requested emails, such as verification codes, scheduled reports, and billing notices;
  • for security: preventing abuse, Two-Step Verification, and auditing data changes;
  • for customer support and fixing errors;
  • for anonymous benchmarks across outlets, only for Merchants that turn on Benchmark Data Consent;
  • to meet legal obligations, such as invoice bookkeeping.

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Who processes data

We use the following service providers, only to the extent required for the related feature. Some providers process data outside Indonesia with equivalent safeguards:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, and file storage;
  • Cloudflare: website and web app hosting, network protection, Turnstile captcha, and encrypted backup storage (R2);
  • Resend and Google: email delivery; Google also for Sign in with Google and the AI receipt reader (Gemini);
  • Midtrans: subscription payments and QRIS;
  • Biteship and courier services: order delivery, if used by the Merchant;
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp Assistant messages, if turned on by the Merchant.

5. Storage and security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) and stored in a database with access rules per Merchant and per role: staff see only the data of the outlet where they work, and a Merchant cannot see another Merchant's data. Merchant Owner and Snouvier Owner accounts must use Two-Step Verification.

Backups are made every night, encrypted before leaving the server, stored outside the main database for 30 days, and tested for restoration every month.

Data is kept while the account is active. Data of a Merchant that stops subscribing is kept for 12 months and then deleted. Data of Customers with no transactions for 3 years is anonymized. After an account deletion request, data is deleted within 30 days, except records that must be kept by law, such as invoice records, which are kept until the retention period ends.

6. Your rights

  • to receive information about and a copy of your personal data;
  • to correct inaccurate data;
  • to request deletion of your account and data (see snouvier-web.celvre.tech/delete-account.html);
  • to withdraw consent and object to certain processing;
  • to file a complaint with the personal data protection authority.

Send requests to the contact address below. We respond within 14 days. Customers of a Merchant should contact that Merchant first, because the Merchant manages their data. Google access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children

Snouvier is intended for businesses and not for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

8. Data breaches

If a personal data protection failure occurs, we notify affected Users and the relevant authority in writing no later than 3 x 24 hours after becoming aware of it, as required by the Personal Data Protection Law. The notice describes the affected data, when and how the incident occurred, and the handling and recovery steps. For Customer data, we notify the Merchant so the Merchant can inform its Customers.

9. Changes to this policy

This Policy may be updated. Material changes are announced in the app or by email before they take effect. The latest version is always available on this page.
