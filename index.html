Features

Everything your outlet needs, every day

Designed with beverage outlets that serve long queues: large buttons, short workflows and reports that are ready the moment you need them.

  • Fast, reliable checkout

    Accept cash, QRIS and customer credit, then print a receipt or send a digital one. Orders can be held and resumed at any time.

  • Accurate ingredient inventory

    Recipes deduct ingredients automatically with every sale. Monitor stock by outlet and warehouse, manage transfers and run stock counts.

  • Automated email reports

    Daily, weekly and monthly reports are delivered to each team member according to their role. No more manual reconciliation at closing time.

  • Multiple outlets, clear roles

    Business Owners see every outlet, while Outlet Admins and Cashiers see only their own. Sensitive actions require approval.

  • Keep selling offline

    Orders are stored on the device when the connection drops and sync automatically once you are back online.

  • Import your catalog from Excel

    Import menus, prices and recipes from an Excel file and be ready to sell within minutes.
