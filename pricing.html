Pricing

Subscription Plans

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial. Pricing is per merchant and covers all outlets and cashiers within your plan limits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

Your account is active for 14 days at no cost as soon as you sign up. After the trial, your subscription continues on the plan you selected.

What happens if a payment is overdue?

There is a 7-day grace period. If the invoice remains unpaid after that, new transactions are paused, but the Business Owner keeps full access to view and export all data.

Could I lose my data?

No. Data is never deleted automatically, and each merchant’s data is fully isolated from other merchants.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. The Business Owner can select a different plan from the Subscription menu. The new plan takes effect once its invoice has been paid.

Pro, or Basic with additional outlets?

Additional outlets increase the outlet quota on the Basic or Pro plan and are billed monthly per outlet together with your subscription; current pricing is shown in the Subscription menu. Choose Basic with an additional outlet if you run two outlets and only need checkout, the catalog and reports. Choose Pro if you run three or more outlets, need more cashiers, or want the kitchen display, customer credit, and recipes with cost of goods.
