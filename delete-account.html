Delete Your Account and Data

Snouvier

You can request deletion of your Snouvier account and its associated data at any time.

How to submit a request

  1. Email privacy@celvre.tech with the subject line "Delete Snouvier account", sent from the email address you use to sign in.
  2. Include your business name (if you are the Business Owner) or the name of the outlet where you work (if you are a staff member).
  3. We will reply within 7 business days to confirm your request, and your account will be deleted within 30 days.

In the app, go to More › Help › Delete account, which opens this page.

What we delete

  • Your sign-in account (email, name and phone number) and personal settings.
  • If the request comes from the Business Owner: all business data, including outlets, menus, stock, transactions, staff and reports.

What we retain temporarily

Transaction and subscription billing records that must be kept under tax regulations are retained until the required retention period ends, and are then deleted. Automated backups are removed within 30 days.
